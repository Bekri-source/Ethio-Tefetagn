Ask a question about your textbook

Pick a subject, then ask about a concept the way you'd ask a study partner. Once your curriculum books are connected on the backend, answers will reference the exact page they came from.

Pick a subject (and, if you like, a grade) above. When that book has been uploaded and indexed, answers are grounded in it with a page citation — otherwise I'll say I'm answering from general knowledge instead.

How page citations work: textbooks live in the Library, sorted by subject and grade. Once a book is uploaded there and indexed (see the project README), this assistant searches it, grounds its answer in the matching page, and cites it — e.g. "Source: Grade 11, page 42" — instead of answering from general knowledge.
